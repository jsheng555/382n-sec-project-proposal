\section{Background}
\label{sec:background}

A side-channel attack recovers information indirectly by observing effects such as execution time, memory access patterns, or cache behavior rather than directly reading protected data.

Modern browsers render webpages by processing HTML/CSS and sending graphical work through the browser's rendering pipeline, often using both the CPU and GPU. During this process, different webpages may share hardware resources such as processor caches and GPU memory.

Pixel-stealing attacks combine these ideas by observing rendering-related timing or cache effects to infer properties of visual content that the attacker is not allowed to directly access.
