\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:evaluation}

We will evaluate the attack primarily based on recovery accuracy and recovery time. Because the victim webpage is generated by us, we will know the correct contents and can directly compare the reconstructed result with the original.

We will measure how accurately individual pixels, regions, or characters can be inferred; how many measurements are required for a reliable inference; and how total recovery time grows as the victim content becomes more complicated.
