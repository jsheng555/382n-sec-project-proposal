\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

Our project is primarily motivated by "Pixel Thief: Exploiting SVG Filter Leakage in Firefox and Chrome" \cite{294651} as well as "GPU.zip: On the Side-Channel Implications of Hardware-Based Graphical Data Compression" \cite{10646718}. These papers show that cache-based and memory-based side channel attacks can be used to leak information from a victim webpage, and will be the basis for our reproduction.
